# Quản lý làm thêm giờ

Columns: Employee, Date, Shift, Requested Start/End, Requested Hours, Actual Hours, OT Type, Approver, Status, Actions. Fixed left `employee,date`; fixed right `status,actions`.

Validate overlap với Shift, Leave, Duty, policy maximum và datetime. API: OvertimeService CRUD; backend cần approve/reject/recalculate actual.
